# Power-BI

This repository features a collection of Power BI projects that demonstrate expertise in data analysis, visualization, and business intelligence. Each project addresses real-world business scenarios, offering actionable insights through dynamic and interactive dashboards.

Project Highlights

Kushi Stores and Sales Dashboard

Analyzed sales trends, customer behavior, and inventory levels.
Provided insights to improve revenue generation and optimize stock management.
Key Visuals: KPI cards and trend lines.

Weather Forecasting Dashboard

Weather Trends: Visualized temperature, precipitation, and humidity trends over time for different locations.
Use Cases: Designed for industries like agriculture, logistics, and travel planning.

Shopping Trends Dashboard

Customer Behavior Analysis: Insights into purchase frequency, average basket size, and preferred shopping times.
Category Performance: Evaluated sales performance by product categories, brands, and regions.
Trend Identification: Highlighted seasonal trends and top-selling products.
Demographic Segmentation: Breakdown of customer profiles based on age, gender, and location for targeted marketing.
Interactive Filters: Enabled drill-down analysis for detailed performance reviews.

Online Sales Dashboard

Sales Performance Monitoring: Key metrics such as total revenue, average order value, and conversion rates.
Real-Time Tracking: Displayed up-to-date sales data with automatic refresh features.
Marketing Impact: Assessed the ROI of marketing campaigns, including referral traffic and ad spend effectiveness.
Regional Performance: Identified top-performing locations and underperforming areas for strategic focus.
Customer Retention Metrics: Monitored repeat purchase rates and customer lifetime value (CLV).

Technologies Used

Power BI: For interactive data visualization and dashboard creation.
Excel: For initial data cleaning and aggregation.
DAX: To create advanced calculations and measures.

Value Delivered

Enabled decision-makers to derive actionable insights from data.
Improved operational efficiency and resource allocation across business functions.
Fostered a culture of data-driven decision-making within organizations.
